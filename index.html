Colour

Colour hierarchy

White carries the layout. Blue leads. Orange stays rare.

White 60%Blue 25%

Orange

Conversion actions, lower states, key numbers. Never for large backgrounds.

Purple

Least-used colour. Scientific graphics, data visualisation, small accents.

Blue and white

These two dominate. Too much blue makes a site read as a generic pharma template.
